DETAILED PROJECT REPORT (DPR)
=====================================
PROJECT TITLE: High-Speed Freight Corridor - Western Sector (Phase II)
AGENCY: Dedicated Freight Corridor Corporation of India Limited (DFCCIL)
DATE: September 2026
-------------------------------------

1. FINANCIAL OVERVIEW
The sanctioned budget for the High-Speed Freight Corridor - Western Sector (Phase II) has been finalized. The Total Budget allocated for this phase is ₹ 14,250 Cr. This includes land acquisition, civil works, electrification, and signalling infrastructure.

2. PROJECT TIMELINE & KEY MILESTONES
The project is scheduled for completion over a 36-month period. The critical path involves several key milestones:
- Environmental Clearance & Land Acquisition Complete (Q1 2027)
- Completion of Earthworks and Track Bed Formation (Q4 2027)
- Foundation Casting and Bridge Erection (Q2 2028)
- Overhead Electrification (OHE) and Signalling Commissioning (Q1 2029)
- Trial Runs and Final Handover (Q3 2029)

3. CONTRACTOR OBLIGATIONS & PENALTIES
The EPC contractor is strictly bound by the following obligations to prevent cost and schedule overruns:
- The contractor must maintain a minimum physical progress of 5% per quarter during the civil works phase.
- Penalty of 0.75% of the contract value per week for schedule slippage beyond a 30-day grace period, capped at a maximum of 10% of the total contract value.
- The contractor is responsible for resolving minor local ROW (Right of Way) disputes without claiming force majeure, unless state intervention is officially delayed by more than 90 days.

4. RISK MANAGEMENT
Primary risks identified include monsoon disruptions in the coastal segments and potential delays in utility shifting. A contingency fund of ₹ 450 Cr has been reserved to address unmapped underground utilities discovered during excavation.
